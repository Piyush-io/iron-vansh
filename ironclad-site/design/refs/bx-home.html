ABOUT The firm: Delivering for Investors

Unmatched scale

Blackstone is the world’s #1 alternative asset manager, with more than $1.3 trillion in AUM. We serve institutional and individual investors by building strong businesses that deliver lasting value. Our scale – with ~13,000 real estate assets and 270+ portfolio companies[1] – enables us to invest in dynamic sectors positioned for long-term growth.
