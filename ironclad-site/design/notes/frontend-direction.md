# Ironclad design direction

Audience: investors assessing an Indian asset manager's public, private, and global equity strategies.
Primary job: explain the mandates and their differences, introduce the team, and make contact easy.

## Tokens

Porcelain #F2F5F7, white #FFFFFF, Atlantic #10354B, graphite #293C46, steel #6B7D85, blue #2A51C9.
IBM Plex Sans throughout. Headlines use weight 500, body 400. A 1.25 type scale provides 16, 20, 25, 31, 39, 49, 61, 76, and 95px reference sizes. Display sizes interpolate for smaller screens. Text stays left aligned, with prose under 70 characters per line.

## Layout

A broad Mumbai photograph establishes the Indian investment context. Three plain links lead to the real investment mandates. The strategy comparison expands on request and exposes the supplied allocation breakdowns. Ownership principles and the founder's real portrait remain quiet supporting sections.

## Review of the first pass

Removed cream and italic serif slogans, decorative numbering, uppercase eyebrow labels, duplicate statements, generic geometric icons, identical photo cards, and arrow decorations. Replaced them with a single type family, cool colors, specific investment language, a geographically relevant photograph, and an interactive mandate comparison. Borders separate actionable rows and real tabular information. Motion only describes interaction.

The previous pass felt like a private-bank template. The revision borrows the clarity of an investment working document while avoiding dense newspaper columns. Its expressive element is the broad skyline and the large, uninterrupted heading. No invented performance statistics or client logos.
